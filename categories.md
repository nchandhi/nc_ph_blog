Progressive Hardening covers six categories. Each one answers a question that grows sharper as the system scales — and each one matures through crawl, walk, and run stages at its own pace.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| [Data](articles/01-data-hardening) | What information can it use? |
| [Security](articles/02-security-hardening) | Who can access it? |
| [Evaluation](articles/03-evaluation-hardening) | How do we know it works? |
| [Platform](articles/04-platform-hardening) | Can it scale reliably? |
| [Automation](articles/05-automation-hardening) | What is it allowed to do? |
| [Governance](articles/06-governance-hardening) | Who is accountable? |

## The Articles

1. **[Data Hardening](articles/01-data-hardening)** — what the system is allowed to know, and why that boundary changes as adoption grows.
2. **[Security Hardening](articles/02-security-hardening)** — who can access the system, what they can do, and how that access is proven and audited.
3. **[Evaluation Hardening](articles/03-evaluation-hardening)** — how quality, safety, and reliability are measured as the system moves from prototype to production.
4. **[Platform Hardening](articles/04-platform-hardening)** — the foundation the system runs on, and how it holds up as usage scales.
5. **[Automation Hardening](articles/05-automation-hardening)** — what the system is allowed to do on its own, and how that authority is bounded.
6. **[Governance Hardening](articles/06-governance-hardening)** — who owns the system, who decides what it does, and who is accountable when something goes wrong.
