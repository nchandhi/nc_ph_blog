# Progressive Hardening Canon

Use this file as the source of truth for all article drafts, talks, and framework assets.

## Thesis

The hardest part is not building the MVP. It is what happens when it works.

## Insight

Success creates new requirements.

## Principle

Progressive Hardening introduces controls and constraints in step with real-world confidence, adoption, and system authority.

## Why This Matters

Teams often optimize for speed at the MVP stage, then struggle when adoption accelerates and risks compound. Progressive Hardening gives teams a way to mature systems intentionally, without over-engineering on day one.

## Dimensions

1. Data
2. Security
3. Evaluation
4. Platform
5. Automation
6. Governance

## Maturity Pattern

- Crawl: low risk, constrained scope, manual oversight
- Walk: expanded scope, stronger controls, repeatable operations
- Run: enterprise scale, formal controls, measurable accountability

## Editorial Voice Rules

- Practical over abstract.
- Opinionated but evidence-based.
- Explain trade-offs explicitly.
- Show what to defer during MVP and what to harden later.

## Canon Check

Before publishing, ask:

- Does this reinforce the thesis?
- Does this clarify one or more dimensions?
- Does this show stage-appropriate controls (crawl, walk, run)?
- Does this avoid day-one perfectionism?
