title: Progressive Hardening Principles
---

## 1. Stage-Appropriate Controls
Do not apply full enterprise controls before there is enterprise exposure. Add controls as risk and reach increase.

## 2. Constraints As Product Features
Controls are not blockers. They are reliability features that preserve trust.

## 3. Adoption Changes Architecture
The architecture that works for 50 users may fail at 5,000 users. Plan for transition points.

## 4. Evaluate Continuously
Evaluation is not a launch checklist. It is an operating loop.

## 5. Human Accountability Persists
Automation can scale execution, but accountability must remain explicit.

## 6. Governance Should Be Legible
Decision rights, escalation paths, and ownership should be clear enough to survive team churn.
